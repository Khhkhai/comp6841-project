QUARTERLY OPERATIONS REPORT
Q2 2026  |  Operations Division

1. Executive Summary
Warehouse throughput rose 12% quarter-on-quarter. The east depot cleared its
backlog two weeks ahead of schedule, while the west depot experienced minor
delays from a short-term staffing shortage that has since been resolved.

2. Safety and Quality
There were zero safety incidents this quarter. Customer complaints fell to a
two-year low, continuing the downward trend from Q1.

3. Outlook
The rollout to the two remaining regional offices remains on schedule. Planning
for the Q3 capacity review has begun, with no major risks currently flagged.

Prepared by the Operations Division for internal review.
